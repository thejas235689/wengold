WENGOLD

Verification that compounds, in the open.

WENGOLD keeps every verification result that still holds after a change, and reruns only what the change could have touched. The graph, the validity kernel and the ledger are open — dual-licensed MIT or Apache-2.0.

After every change: which of my previous results still hold? Valid, invalidated, or unknown — never assumed.
